About Us

Lutong Bayan is a Filipino restaurant that serves delicious, affordable, and traditional Filipino meals. We believe that every dish should bring the familiar taste and comfort of home.

Our goal is to provide satisfying meals while celebrating Filipino food and culture.

Our Mission

To become a trusted Filipino restaurant known for for authentic home-cooked meals and excelent service.
